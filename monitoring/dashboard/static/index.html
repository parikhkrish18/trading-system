▸ What do these terms mean? (feature glossary)
TermHow it is calculatedWhat the value means for the stock
mom_ret_5d — 5-day momentum return (Price today / Price 5 trading days ago) − 1 Positive: stock has risen over the last week. Negative: stock has fallen. Larger absolute values mean stronger recent price movement.
mom_ret_20d — 20-day momentum return (Price today / Price 20 trading days ago) − 1 Same idea over roughly one month. Comparing it with mom_ret_5d shows whether short-term momentum agrees with or is reversing the broader recent trend.
adx_14 — 14-day Average Directional Index Calculated from 14-day smoothed positive and negative directional movement (+DI and −DI), then ADX = smoothed average of 100 × abs(+DI − −DI) / (+DI + −DI) Measures trend strength, not direction. Roughly: <20 = weak/choppy, 20–25 = developing trend, >25 = meaningful trend, >40 = strong trend. It does not say whether the trend is bullish or bearish.
vol_realized_20d — 20-day realized volatility Standard deviation of the last 20 daily returns, annualized: std(daily returns) × √252 How violently the stock has actually been moving. Higher = larger/more unpredictable daily swings. Lower = calmer price behavior. Does not indicate direction.
vol_atr_14 — 14-day Average True Range Daily True Range = max(High−Low, abs(High−PrevClose), abs(Low−PrevClose)), then its 14-day Wilder average Typical daily movement in dollars. ATR of $5 means a normal day's total movement is around $5. Useful for stops and position sizing, not bullish/bearish direction.
vol_of_vol — volatility regime ratio Recent 10-day realized volatility / 60-day realized volatility >1: stock has recently become more volatile than normal. <1: it has become calmer. ≈1: recent volatility is similar to its longer-term norm.
meanrev_zscore_20d — 20-day price z-score (Current price − 20-day mean price) / 20-day standard deviation of price Measures how unusually far price is from its recent average. +2: roughly 2 standard deviations above normal. −2: roughly 2 below. Large magnitudes indicate a stretched price, though they can also signal a genuine breakout.
meanrev_bollinger_pctb — Bollinger %B Upper band = 20-day MA + 2σ; lower = 20-day MA − 2σ; %B = (Price − Lower) / (Upper − Lower) 1 = upper Bollinger Band, 0.5 = 20-day average, 0 = lower band. >1 means price broke above the normal band; <0 means it broke below it.
meanrev_rsi_14 — 14-day RSI 14-period average gains and losses; RS = Avg Gain / Avg Loss; RSI = 100 − 100/(1+RS) Measures recent buying vs. selling momentum on a 0–100 scale. Roughly >70 = overbought/strong upward momentum, <30 = oversold/strong downward momentum, ~50 = balanced.
Regime — market trend/chop regime Calculate ADX-14 for SPY. If ADX ≥ 25, label market Trending; otherwise label Choppy. Gives every stock market context. Trending: momentum-type signals may be more reliable. Choppy: breakouts may fail more often and mean-reversion behavior may matter more.
sentiment_mean_3d — 3-day company-news sentiment Score each relevant company headline/article from −1 to +1, then average scores from the last 3 days Positive: recent news backdrop is favorable. Negative: recent news is unfavorable. Near zero means neutral/mixed news.
sentiment_mean_10d — 10-day company-news sentiment Average the stock's news sentiment scores from the last 10 days Gives a slower-moving news baseline. Useful for distinguishing a one-day news shock from a sustained positive or negative narrative.
sentiment_momentum_3v10 — sentiment momentum sentiment_mean_3d − sentiment_mean_10d Positive: news has recently become more bullish than its prior baseline. Negative: news is deteriorating. Near zero means sentiment hasn't materially changed.
news_volume_3d — 3-day news volume Count of relevant company-news events/articles during the previous 3 days Measures how much information is arriving. High volume: something significant may be happening and the sentiment estimate has more observations. Low volume: sentiment may depend on only one or two stories.
macro_mkt_sentiment — broad-market news sentiment Recency-weighted aggregation of sentiment from broad-market news tied to instruments such as SPY, QQQ, Dow and Russell 2000 Positive: broad news environment is supportive/risk-on. Negative: market backdrop is bearish/risk-off. Provides context for company-specific news.
macro_sector_sentiment — sector news sentiment Recency-weighted aggregation of sentiment for the stock's sector, e.g. XLK/technology for a technology company Shows whether the company's sector has a favorable or unfavorable news backdrop. Helps separate company-specific strength from sector-wide strength.
macro_mkt_x_own_sentiment — market × stock sentiment interaction macro_mkt_sentiment × stock's own sentiment Large positive: stock news and market backdrop reinforce one another. Negative: stock-specific news and market sentiment disagree. Near zero means one or both signals are neutral.
macro_sector_x_own_sentiment — sector × stock sentiment interaction macro_sector_sentiment × stock's own sentiment Large positive: company and sector news point the same way. Negative: company is bucking its sector's sentiment. Useful for detecting confirmation vs. divergence.
days_to_next_fomc — days until FOMC Calendar difference between today and the next scheduled Federal Reserve interest-rate decision Small number: major macro event risk is close, so volatility and uncertainty may rise. Large number: less immediate FOMC-related event risk.
days_to_next_cpi — days until CPI Calendar days until the next scheduled U.S. CPI inflation release Small number: inflation-driven market risk is imminent. Especially relevant to rate-sensitive/growth stocks.
days_to_next_jobs — days until jobs report Calendar days until the next major U.S. employment report, typically Nonfarm Payrolls Small number: employment/rate-expectation volatility is near. Large number: jobs-event risk is farther away.
fund_eps_actual_latest — latest diluted EPS Most recently publicly reported diluted EPS available as of the observation date Profit attributable to each diluted share. Higher/positive EPS generally means stronger profitability, but the change and surprise versus expectations often matter more than the raw value.
fund_revenue_actual_latest — latest quarterly revenue Most recent publicly filed quarterly total revenue How much the company sold during its latest reported quarter. Higher revenue isn't automatically bullish; revenue growth and performance versus expectations matter more.
fund_net_income_latest — latest quarterly net income Most recent publicly reported quarterly revenue minus all expenses, interest, taxes, etc. The company's bottom-line profit. Positive/rising: generally healthier profitability. Negative/falling: losses or deteriorating profitability, though context matters.
fund_gross_profit_latest — latest gross profit Revenue − Cost of Goods Sold (COGS), using the latest reported quarter Money remaining after the direct cost of producing what the company sells. Rising gross profit can indicate growth, pricing power, or better economics; falling gross profit can signal pressure.
fund_total_assets_latest — latest total assets Most recent reported balance-sheet total of cash, investments, receivables, inventory, property, intangibles, etc. Shows the size of the company's economic resource base. More assets are not automatically bullish; their quality and the returns generated from them matter.
fund_total_liabilities_latest — latest total liabilities Most recent reported balance-sheet total of debts and other obligations Shows how much the company owes. High/rising liabilities can mean greater financial risk, but should be compared with assets, cash flow, earnings and the nature of those liabilities.
